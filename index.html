What it does

⌨ Types into anything

Your words arrive as real typing in whatever has your cursor — email, chat apps, documents, browsers, code editors. No per-app setup, no pasting.

🫧 Live preview bubble

Watch your sentence build as you speak, with each phrase color-coded so you can see exactly what just landed before it's committed.

🗣 Hands-free wake phrases

Say "start dictating" to begin and "stop dictating" to end — no hotkey, no hands. Perfect mid-task, mid-sandwich, mid-anything.

🔒 Fully offline engine

Flip on the embedded Whisper engine and everything is transcribed right on your PC — no internet, nothing leaves your machine.

🔊 Dictate what's playing

Point it at an output device and transcribe whatever your PC is playing — calls, videos, podcasts, meetings.

✨ Smart formatting

A local AI politely rewrites your rambling into clean, send-ready text — and it can read the box you're typing in so it continues your thought naturally.

↶ Voice undo

Say "scratch that" and the last phrase is removed — including from the target box, verified before a single backspace is sent. "Start over" wipes it all.

🎙️ Smart mic

Every connected mic is metered live and dictation follows whichever hears you loudest. Walk from your desk mic to your headset mid-sentence.

⏳ Catch-up typing

Dictate first, click a text box after — the held text types itself the moment focus lands. With smart paste, it appends only what's missing, never duplicating.

📸 Snip OCR

A bonus hotkey snips any text on your screen — a screenshot, a video, an error message — straight to your clipboard as text. Free, forever.

📜 Dictation history

Cycle back through everything you've dictated, with date and time, and copy or paste any of it again.

📋 Clipboard history

The tray remembers your recent snips and dictations for one-click re-copying when you need that thing from ten minutes ago.

Smart Recover: the dictation that never gets lost (free)

Talked for five minutes, closed the bubble, lost it all? One tap brings your last dictation right back. It's always kept safe on your PC — free for everyone, because losing five minutes of thinking out loud should never happen to anybody.

How it works

  1. Download and run. The exe installs itself per-user in about a second. No wizard, no admin prompt.
  2. Put your cursor anywhere and press the hotkey — or just say "start dictating."
  3. Talk. Words land live where your cursor is. Say "submit" to send it, "scratch that" to fix the last phrase, "stop dictating" when you're done.

Say it, don't click it

"start dictating" Begin, hands-free
"stop dictating" End the session
"submit" Send what you've dictated
"scratch that" Remove the last phrase
"start over" Wipe it and start fresh

Questions

Is it free?

You get 14 days of everything, no account needed. After that the everyday core stays free forever: streaming dictation with your mic, live typing, punctuation, auto-caps, voice submit, snip OCR, and Smart Recover. A one-time Pro license unlocks the power lane: the offline engine, system-audio dictation, smart formatting, wake phrases, voice undo, smart mic, and the full dictation history.

Does my voice leave my PC?

With the Pro offline engine, no — transcription runs entirely on your machine and works with the internet off. The free streaming engine transcribes through the built-in browser speech service. Either way, we never see or store your dictations; the app's only phone-home is an anonymous update check you can switch off.

Can it really type into anything?

Yes. It sends real keystrokes to whatever has focus, so anywhere you could type, it can type — chat apps, email, documents, browsers, terminals, code editors.

Windows said "Windows protected your PC"?

That's SmartScreen being cautious with a new, unsigned app. Click More info, then Run anyway. The app installs per-user (no admin prompt) and uninstalls cleanly from Settings → Apps.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and a microphone. The download bundles everything else — the offline engine's models are built in.

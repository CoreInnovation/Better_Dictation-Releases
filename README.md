# Better Dictation — Download

**Speak anywhere.** Better Dictation lives in your Windows system tray: press a hotkey (or just say
"start dictating") and your words are typed live into whatever you're focused on — any app, any text box.

## Install (10 seconds, no admin)

1. Download **[BetterDictation.exe](../../releases/latest/download/BetterDictation.exe)**
2. Run it. That's it — it installs itself to your account (no admin prompt), adds Start-menu
   shortcuts, and starts with Windows.

Updates are automatic (and free). Uninstall anytime from Windows Settings → Apps.

## Highlights

- **Live dictation bubble** — see your words as you say them, with voice undo ("scratch that")
- **Hands-free** — wake phrases start/stop dictation with no hotkey at all (Pro)
- **Fully offline engine** — embedded Whisper: no internet, nothing ever leaves your PC (Pro)
- **System-audio dictation** — transcribe what's *playing* on your PC: calls, videos, podcasts (Pro)
- **Smart mic** — walk between microphones mid-sentence; dictation follows your voice (Pro)
- **AI polish** — a local LLM turns rambles into clean, send-ready text (Pro)
- **Bonus**: screen-snip OCR to clipboard, clipboard history

Full-featured **14-day free trial**, then a generous free tier. One-time purchase — no subscriptions, ever.

*Privacy: dictation never leaves your PC (offline engine) or goes only to your browser's speech service
(streaming engine). The only network call the app itself makes is an anonymous update check.*
